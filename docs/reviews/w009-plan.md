## How the weights are picked (must be crystal clear)
- Box: the reference lab's wooden transport box, glass lid, ONE row of ten velvet compartments, no numbers inside (it is the lab's
  general box). A brass clasp at one end; the seal (no. ۵۵۱۸) goes through the clasp. Compartments are counted from the clasp end.
- Weights: set «م», ten polished steel 100 g cylinders, identical to eye and hand; م-۱ = 100.000 g and each next one is 4 mg heavier
  (م-۱۰ = 100.036 g). Nothing is engraved on them.
- Frame (Radin's drawing, e3): a cradle on the bench; the box sits in it only one way (clasp into the notch on the RIGHT). Along the
  front lip, under the row of compartments, a clear card rail OUTSIDE the box and its seal. One card stands under each compartment.
  Cards slide in from the LEFT end; a knurled end screw at the left end, turned by hand, closes the rail. After tightening, the
  installer draws a blue lacquer line across the screw head and the rail; if the screw turns, the line breaks.
- Test: the seal is broken, the lid opened; the operator takes the weight of compartment 1 (clasp end) with tweezers, reads the card
  under that compartment aloud, types the card's mass; the instrument weighs and prints «typed / measured»; then compartment 2 ...
  10. Pass: every row within 1 mg. The operator never knows a weight's name except from the card under it.

## Numbers (e2, e4)
Compartment k (from the clasp end) holds م-k = 100.000 + 4(k-1) mg. After the reversal the card under compartment k is م-(11-k).
Row k: typed = 100.036 - 4(k-1); measured = 100.000 + 4(k-1); difference = measured - typed = 8(k-1) - 36 mg:
-36, -28, -20, -12, -4, +4, +12, +20, +28, +36. All ten fail (limit 1 mg). It looks like a growing «slope error» (option m1), but the
measured column is exactly the packing slip and the typed column is the slip read backwards.
