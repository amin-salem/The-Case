### Changes from the idea (and why)
- **Title** «دسته‌ای که همراه ساز رفت» -> «دسته‌ای که برگشت»: in the rebuilt chain the handle does not go with the instrument; it goes back to the case it came from, and that return trip is the whole trick.
- **Names** (every first name and surname is new to the repo's suspects): پریسا ماهان -> گیسو تاجبخش, هوشنگ مهرپرور -> نوذر سمندری, بهزاد کامیار -> ایمان رزمجو, بابک رهنما -> خشایار فخاری, آزاده نوری -> منصوره دیبایی. Non-suspects: استاد اسحاق دلنشین (luthier), ذبیح گلشنی (stage-door guard), شهروز ملکان (lighting technician), شکوه افراسیابی (curator of the foundation's collection), آقای جهان‌بین (hall manager), آقای میرشکار (lender, money changer in the bazaar), «تالار آوا» (rival hall). The instrument is «تار ۰۷» of the collection of بنیاد چکامه (owner of تالار چکامه).
- **The two cases are introduced in the intro, with every difference**: Q = the tar's own case (brown leather over wood, brass corners and four brass feet, green velvet shaped to the tar, brass plate «۰۷» inside the lid, original handle cracked and tied with red string); M = the spare case (black fibreglass, rubber feet, grey foam, healthy black leather handle with chrome rings, fixed with short chrome machine screws into metal inserts). Both always travel in identical grey canvas covers with a slit for the handle, so from outside a covered case is known only by its handle.
- **Weight card dropped** (the idea's "weight at delivery"): Iman (Behzad) is cleared by two people (guard, lighting technician) and by what he says himself, not by a machine. Only one machine-like card: e2 (photo).
- **Houshang's ownership claim** kept as Nozar's lie; the foundation file (e8) shows he sold the tar in 1398 (his secret), so he has no claim, and the guard and the hall manager's foyer put him away from the cases.
- **Real motive made concrete:** on 12 Mehr Khashayar took tar 07 out of the foundation vault «for the soloist's rehearsals» and pledged it to the lender Mirshekar for 300 million toman. Gisu first saw the tar on Saturday 5 Dey (her answer). He got it back for one night, promising it by Monday 7 Dey 7:15 in the back alley (Mansoureh's answer: the lender phoned her house). Checklist (e7): the stage manager opens the hall at 7:00; at 7:30 Iman drives the spare case to the foundation warehouse. Surface motive (everyone's belief): his 150-million debt to Nozar.
- Culprit slot s4 (recent: c088 s3, c089 s5, c090 s5).
- Difficulty set to 4 (an S2 case whose "how" leads to "who"; also keeps `test_mostly_difficulty_four` true: c092 is 5).

### Clue chain (intended solution)
1. **The handle is the only outside sign of a covered case** (intro) and it moves only with a screwdriver.
2. **Where the handle was.** 17:25: black handle from M onto Q with two long brass wood screws; M's own short chrome screws in the luthier's box (e3). 21:50: the black handle is on Q, which holds the tar (e2). 23:12: the black handle is on M, held by the same brass wood screws, badly (e1). 23:35: Q has no handle and two empty holes with fresh brass dust; the tar is inside (e4). So after 21:50 the handle went back from Q to M: the case that went into the loan room was the empty spare; the tar was never in the room.
3. **When and where.** From 17:30 M stood on the lower shelf of the stage manager's desk; from 21:55 Q stood on the same desk (e6). At 22:15 the handle-less case Iman took from the lower shelf was already Q: it went to the cage (e4) and was heavier (Iman's answer); the brass feet scratched the lower shelf, which M's rubber feet cannot do (e5). So the handle went back between 21:55 and 22:15, at the desk.
4. **Who.** Only Khashayar went behind the desk; he lifted the case off the desk top at 22:05 and put «a case with a handle» back at 22:11; he did not leave the desk until 22:30 (e6). Iman was on stage until 22:14 (e9); Mansoureh only passed; Gisu left at 21:55; Nozar was in the foyer (e6).
5. **His lies:** «the tar case never left my desk top» (e6); «I never touched the spare» (e1 + e3 + e5); «at 22:08 I went to check the strike» (e6, e9).
6. **Real motive:** e8 (vault out 12 Mehr «برای تمرین تک‌نواز») + Gisu's answer (first held it on 5 Dey) + Mansoureh's answer (the lender's call: Monday 7:15 behind the hall) + e7 (stage manager opens at 7:00; 7:30 the spare goes to the warehouse with Iman).
