## Changes against the idea
- Names. The idea's invented first names (کامران‌فر, بهنام‌فر, هوشنگیار, سهرابک, رامین‌مهر) were replaced by natural ones that do
  not clash with the cases of the same fortnight (c085 کامرانی, c086 بهنام/فرزان, c088 رامین, c089 سهراب, c076 امیرهوشنگ, c084 قاسم,
  c082 جاسم, w008 نوذری): culprit **سیروس دادبه**; his sister **نادیا دادبه** (siblings share the surname; the idea's نگین is
  also overused); **بابک جاوید** (appraiser); **ابراهیم رادپور** (trustee); **صابر کاوندی** (porter); **تورج سلیمی** (courier).
  Kept: **فریناز مهرپویا**, **آذرنوش بهروان**. Non-suspects: ناخدا **یحیی دریاگرد** (captain of the lenj «نسیم», Nadia's secret
  husband), تجارتخانه‌ی **سارای** (seller, across the sea), **بازرگانی سپیددشت** (consignee = Siroos's hidden company),
  تجارتخانه‌ی **دریانور** (Fariinaz + Siroos), «استاد رادپور» (Ebrahim's late father, a bookbinder; Azarnoush's master).
- Relations made coherent: Nadia is Siroos's younger sister and Touraj's colleague (both staff of Daryanavard), a university
  friend of Babak (he was the witness at her secret عقد). Ebrahim is Fariinaz's maternal uncle (hidden: e6 clause 4 forbids the
  trustee to release a relative's cargo). Babak is Ebrahim's apprentice (not licensed: his last exam is unfinished, so Ebrahim countersigns); Saber is Touraj's friend; Azarnoush was Ebrahim's
  father's apprentice.
- Receipts/forms: the idea cleared the manager's debt with a settlement document and the courier's stop with a repair receipt.
  Both are converted: the debt by a person (Azarnoush's answer: Yalda night, Ebrahim tore the cheque in front of her; no paper
  left, as Fariinaz also says) and the stop by objects (e15: fresh tyre plug, chalk ring, the nail, a girl's school bag) plus the
  camera line. Forms that clear anybody: only e2 (the countersigned assessment note: contract present until 12:25) and, partly, e7
  (reservation card: Nadia was not at her desk). Two at most, as asked.
- The chain kept: delivery photo (strap direction) → assessment note (contract present) → carry tag + the two carriers' words
  (opening limited to the stop at Siroos's desk) → torn lining piece in his personal carton. Rebuilt so that no card alone
  decides (see "Why no single card decides").

## The trick («آهان»)
The strap is sewn to the middle of the back cover and can reach the front around either edge. The seller wraps it around the
OPEN edge (left, the folder's spine is on the right): then the word «سارای» stamped near its tip reads upright (e1). Wrapped
around the SPINE edge, the strap arrives from the other side and the word is upside down (e4, e13). A correct re-wrap leaves no
trace (Azarnoush's answer), so the strap does not show every opening, only the one where somebody wrapped it wrongly. The carry
tag's NAMES are wrong twice (Nadia's line is in Touraj's hand; Siroos is not on it at all), the strap is not.

## Background (true)
- 1399: Fariinaz and Siroos open «تجارتخانه‌ی دریانور» (carpet samples).
- Mehr 1405: Fariinaz borrows 300 M from her uncle Ebrahim (family loan) to pay Siroos's half of a purchase.
- 12 آبان: Siroos orders from Azarnoush a rubber stamp «بازرگانی سپیددشت، انبار ۱۴ ردیف ج» and a leather ledger; pays cash;
  collects them himself on 19 آبان (e14). He rents warehouse 14 row C in that name.
- 14 آبان: Azarnoush sells the hand press and two brass dies of her late master (Ebrahim's father), left with her in trust (e14).
- 20 آبان: contract with «سارای»: 12 silk sample rugs (50×70 cm), 640 M تومان; Fariinaz pays all 640 (her 320 + 320 lent to
  Siroos). Clause 6, written at her request: «تا پرداخت سهم آقای سیروس دادبه از بها، سیصد و بیست میلیون تومان، به خانم فریناز
  مهرپویا، مالکیت نمونه‌ها با خانم مهرپویاست و ترخیص فقط با امضای او». Six sheets, Persian and Russian, stapled at the top corner.
  The seller ships the only original with the goods (e6: copies are not accepted for release).
- 18 آذر: Siroos books the shipment by phone for Daryanavard, consignee «بازرگانی سپیددشت»; tells Fariinaz it is a customs
  broker's name (her answer). Babak: no such broker in this port.
- Mehr: Nadia's secret عقد with captain Yahya Daryagard; Siroos, head of the family since their father died, had forbidden it.
- 28 آذر: the lenj «نسیم» loads at the seller's port.
- Mon ۳۰ آذر (Yalda night): at Ebrahim's house Fariinaz and her mother (Ebrahim's sister) repay the 300 M in cash; he tears her
  cheque. Azarnoush, who had come with an album she had rebound (and to confess about the tools; she did not), sees it.

## Day 1 vs day 2
- Day 1 (e1-e11): a very careful player can see that the strap was reversed in Saber's twenty minutes, that the only stop was
  Siroos's desk and that Siroos lies about it, and that clause 6 + rule 11 + an unknown consignee give a motive. Doubts that stay:
  Saber could have opened it himself and lied about «upright»; the 13:41 «upside down» is the word of the creditor-uncle (and
  Fariinaz, who is in the collusion trail); an earlier opener (Touraj's half hour, the captain next to Nadia's desk) could have
  taken the contract and re-wrapped correctly, leaving the later wrong wrap as a mere peek; Sepiddasht's owner is unknown.
  So: a leaning on Siroos, not certainty. m2 is reachable for the careful one (Babak: no such broker; Nadia: he goes to the
  warehouses; his own «we'll get along with Sepiddasht»).
- Day 2 (e12-e16, Azarnoush): proof, not essential new facts: an independent witness to the 13:41 state and the single fresh
  crease (e13), the triangle in carton 2 riding on the staple (e12, e13), the contract burnt in the consignee's warehouse (e16),
  Sepiddasht's stamp ordered by Siroos (e14), the van explained (e15), the debt paid (Azarnoush's answer).
