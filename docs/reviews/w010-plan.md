## Culprit, trick, motive
- Culprit: **s3 توماج روشن‌آرا**, 41, stage assistant (portrait m_neutral_45_reserve22, never a culprit). Not the newcomer.
- Trick («آهان»): the most precise witness of the folder's journey (all his times match the cue log) leaves out the last hand: his
  own. He moves the folder to Kiana's shelf, puts himself in the rope recess «until the blue light», and says the folder reached

  (field): romantic revenge (he proposed last year and was refused).
