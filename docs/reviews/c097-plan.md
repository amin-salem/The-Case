## The trick («آهان»)
Eight plates existed, eight guests did not. Every real guest leaves traces: a chair, a place card, a plate served at a seat, a dirty plate back.
There are 60 of each. The eight "guests" left only eight takeaway packs, which by the hall's rule go to the group's reception desk.

## Money
Hall price 1.4M a plate: 68 x 1.4M = 95.2M, paid at 23:00 from the group fund card (e1). The fund lost 8 x 1.4M = 11.2M.
Ahoo sold the 8 packs at the desk at 1.5M each = 12M cash, receipts 31-36 in the group's pad (e6), and kept the cash. The 12M is exactly what she
paid from her own pocket for the broken second bus on the Kashan trip, never repaid (e8). The fund box she handed Keyvan at 22:45 held only
the raffle-ticket money, 3.2M (Keyvan's answer). The 12M stays with her (the explanation says so; no card is needed for where it is now).
