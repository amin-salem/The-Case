## The single answer (fix from the batch-3 notes)
The question is: «چه کسی نقشه را کشید و پاکت را با دست خودش از دفتر برداشت؟» The answer is **Ramtin (s1)** and only Ramtin:
he made up the errand that put the key in Pedram's hand, he took the key from Pedram, opened the office at 22:20, hid the envelope and
the donation book, tied the bundle and asked to ride to town with the laundry van, and only he has a motive (the book would expose his skimming).
**Pedram (s3) is a knowing accomplice** (borrowed the key on a false errand, lied that it never left his pocket, covered the desk and told the
"toilet" story) but he never entered the office and gains nothing; he is never the answer. The intro and the question say "planned and took
with his own hands"; the explanation says Pedram helped knowingly but did not plan or take.
