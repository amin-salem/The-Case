### Clue chain (intended solution)
1. **Absence** (e1): of 11 trips only trip 9's charter was never received; the row is written in advance (12/5), «معاف ـ به خاطر عصمت». Ghafour wrote down even Yavar's 600,000 cargo fee.
2. **Why exempt** (e2): clause 7, only for the owner's family. So Esmat is family and the trip was taken in a family member's name.
3. **Who is family** (e3): Esmat is Ghafour's sister; her son, 32, back from Kish after two years in a hotel kitchen, works every week on Shahbaz with Ashraf, under his father's surname. Of the two men on Shahbaz: Yavar 37, never left Nilgun (statement); Kurosh 32 then (50 now), says he spent five years in Kish (statement). Confirmed by Nazila's answer (Kurosh's mother is Esmat, who always said her brother owns a boat) and Kurosh's own answer (mother from Khorshid, died last year: same as Ghafour's sister).
4. **Who arranged it** (e9 + e7 + Ghafour's answer): on 12 Mordad Kurosh himself arranged trip 9 with Zahedi; the same afternoon a man was behind Ghafour's closed door and the row was written; Ghafour: his nephew asked for the trip «as his own».
5. **The declared expense** (e5 + e4): Kurosh took 8M «for the boat» from Nazila on 19 Mordad and declared it paid by hand to Ghafour at 6:45 on 20 Mordad.
6. **It was never paid**: the contract pays only in the office against a signature (e2); his receipt has no owner signature (e4); Sadra was not «right there» at 6:45 but in the cabin with Yavar (e7, e6 signature 6:40); at 6:50 Kurosh's hands are empty (e8); the book has no payment (e1); Ghafour: nobody gave him money that day.
7. **Aha**: the man who knew the boat was free (he got it free from his uncle) is the one who charged the company 8M for it. What was not paid exposes what was declared.
8. **Real motive**: e9 (5 Tir teachers, Kish trip) + Nazila's answer (repay by end of Mordad, «paid» at end of Mordad) + Ashraf's answer (Nakhaei's call; Sepidbal never sold Kish) + e3 (kitchen, not guide).

Proof (contradict Kurosh's own words): e1 (paid), e2 (cash at dock allowed), e3 (no relation, Kish guide), e7 (Sadra right there): 4 of 9.
