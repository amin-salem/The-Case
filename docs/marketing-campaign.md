# Marketing campaign plan («پرونده»)

Saved 15 Oct 2026 at Amin's request, so we can talk about it later. This is a draft plan, not a decision.
Prices and platform rules are from memory: check the current rates before spending.

## 0. Open questions (Amin has not answered yet)
1. Total budget for the first month?
2. Cafe Bazaar only, or Myket too?

## 1. Before any spending: fix the funnel
- Invite codes must work (scheduled task, 15 Oct night: onboarding step + honest card).
- Bazaar listing ready: icon, screenshots, video, texts (done earlier).
- Know the numbers first: cost per install, day-1 and day-7 return, share of players who buy coins.
  Without them we cannot tell if a campaign pays.

## 2. Week 1: cheap channels (small amounts, 3-4 channels)
- Cafe Bazaar ads (pay per install): best targeted installs. Start tiny, one category (puzzles / thinking games).
- Telegram channels: mystery, crime, story, entertainment. A medium channel with the right audience beats a big generic one.
  Use the 8-second scene videos.
- Instagram and Rubika: 15-30 s clips, «پرونده امشب رو حل کن».
- Small influencers (nano/micro): crime/film/series/puzzle pages. Not big names first.

## 3. Content that grows by itself
- A free daily puzzle shareable without installing, with the download link under it.
- «تو هم قاتل رو پیدا کردی؟» posts with the percentage of detectives who guessed right.
- Weekly leaderboard and its prize shown on social media.
- Real player ratings and comments (with permission).

## 4. Budget rule
- 70 % across 3-4 channels with small amounts, wait 3-5 days.
- Raise the budget on the channel with the lowest cost per install and best day-7 return.
- Keep 30 % for the winner.

## 5. Do not
- No bought installs or fake reviews (Bazaar detects them and ranks the app lower).
- Do not spend everything in one day: players who do not come back bring no profit.
