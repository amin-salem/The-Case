# Avatar image prompts (16 exclusive avatars)

How to use: copy the **full prompt** of one avatar into your image tool, make the picture, then send me the files named exactly as in the first column (`ex_xxx.png`, 1024×1024). I convert them to webp and wire them into the app.

**Midjourney:** add `--ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6.1 --s 150` at the end. **Other tools:** use square 1:1, 1024×1024. Keep the SAME tool and settings for all 16 so they look like one set (and like the 12 free detectives).

**Negative prompt** (if your tool has one; in Midjourney put it after `--no`):

> text, letters, numbers, watermark, signature, logo, flag, religious symbol, gore, blood, weapon pointed at viewer, bright saturated colors, cartoon, anime, 3d render, photo, extra people, extra fingers, deformed face, cropped head

**Rules:** fictional people only, no text/letters/numbers anywhere, no religious or political symbols, women wear hijab (roosari), modest clothing, nothing gory. If a result shows writing, a weapon aimed at the viewer or a real-looking celebrity, regenerate.

The prompts already include the style block and the portrait part. Don't add anything else.
